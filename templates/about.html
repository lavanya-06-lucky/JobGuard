About JobGuard

A smarter way to evaluate online job postings and identify potentially suspicious opportunities.

What is JobGuard?

JobGuard is an AI-assisted job verification system designed to help job seekers evaluate online job postings before applying.

The system analyzes information such as job title, company details, description, salary, experience, contact information and job links.

Based on these details, JobGuard generates a risk score and highlights suspicious indicators that users should verify before proceeding.

What JobGuard Provides

🤖

AI-Assisted Analysis

Uses machine learning and text analysis to identify patterns commonly associated with suspicious job postings.

⚠️

Risk Indicators

Highlights indicators such as payment requests, unusual salary claims, messaging-only communication and shortened links.

📊

Risk Score

Provides a simple risk score to help users understand how carefully a job opportunity should be verified.

📝

Job Reports

Users can report suspicious job postings and provide information that can help improve awareness.

🗄️

Secure Database

Job analysis records and submitted reports are stored in a MySQL database for organized management.

🛡️

Safety Guidance

Provides practical safety tips to help users recognize suspicious recruitment practices before sharing money or personal information.

Important

JobGuard provides an AI-assisted risk assessment. It does not guarantee that a job posting is genuine or fraudulent. Users should independently verify the employer, recruitment process and job details before sharing personal information or making any payment.
