JOB SAFETY GUIDE

Stay Safe While Job Hunting

Learn how to identify common warning signs and verify job opportunities before applying.

WARNING SIGNS

Common Signs of Suspicious Job Offers

One warning sign does not automatically prove that a job is fraudulent. Look at the overall situation and verify the employer independently.

💰

Upfront Payment

Be careful if a recruiter asks you to pay registration, training, processing or application fees before getting a job.

📱

Messaging-Only Contact

Be cautious when recruitment happens only through WhatsApp, Telegram or personal messaging accounts without verifiable company information.

💸

Unrealistic Salary

Extremely high income promises for simple work or applicants with no experience should be carefully verified.

🔗

Suspicious Links

Check shortened URLs and unfamiliar websites carefully before opening links or entering personal information.

🏢

Missing Company Information

Verify the company name, official website, office details and careers page independently.

⏰

Pressure to Act Quickly

Be cautious if someone pressures you to pay money or share personal information immediately without giving you time to verify the offer.

PROTECT YOUR INFORMATION

Never Share Sensitive Information

🔐

Passwords

Never share your account passwords with recruiters or unknown people.

🔢

OTPs

Never share OTPs received on your phone, even if someone claims to be a recruiter.

💳

Banking Details

Do not provide card numbers, PINs or other banking credentials to suspicious recruiters.

🪪

Identity Documents

Share identity documents only when necessary and with a verified organization through a trusted process.

VERIFY BEFORE APPLYING

A Simple Verification Checklist

1

Find the Official Company Website

Search for the company's official website independently instead of relying only on a link sent by the recruiter.

2

Check the Careers Page

Check whether the same job vacancy appears on the company's official careers page.

3

Verify the Recruiter

Check whether the recruiter's email and professional identity can be independently verified.

4

Never Pay to Get a Job

Treat requests for upfront payments as a serious warning sign and verify the offer independently.

5

Compare the Job Details

Compare salary, qualifications, location and responsibilities with information available from trusted sources.
